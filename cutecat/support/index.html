Quick help

I can't hear the sounds.

Check your iPhone's media volume and audio output. Open Sound, enable the room sound or Continuous purring, and raise its individual level. If the timer has finished or headphones were disconnected, turn your sounds on again.

The audio stops when I leave the app.

Audio behavior can differ between beta builds. Please email your CuteCat version, iOS version, and whether you use the iPhone speaker or headphones. You can keep the app open while we investigate.

How do I change my cat or room?

In the latest beta, swipe left or right across the room. Each cat has its own atmosphere. If your beta has a Cats button, use it to choose a companion. Your selection is saved on your iPhone.

How does the sleep timer work?

Open Sound and choose Turn sounds off after. At the end, the sounds fade out. Choose Off to cancel the timer. Stop all stops the sounds and cancels the timer. Sleep and Wake change your cat’s pose; they are separate from the timer.

A tip didn't go through.

Tips are optional. If products do not load, check your internet connection and try again later. A pending purchase may need Apple's or a parent's approval. You can keep using CuteCat while a purchase is pending.

How can I request a refund?

Apple processes in-app purchases. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to check your purchase and request a refund. Eligibility depends on Apple's policies and applicable law.

How can I remove my data?

CuteCat has no account to delete. Companion and sound preferences are stored locally. Use Delete App in iOS to remove local app data, and manage backups through your device or Apple settings. To request deletion of support correspondence, email us. More details are in our Privacy Policy.

Report a problem

Tell us which cat you selected, what you tapped, what you expected, and what happened. Include your app version and device details. For visual issues, a short recording can help. Please leave out passwords, payment details, and private notifications.
