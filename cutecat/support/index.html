Quick help

I can't hear the sounds.

Check your iPhone's media volume and audio output. Tap Sound, enable the room sound or Continuous purring, and raise its individual volume. If the timer has finished, turn your sounds on again. Check whether audio is being sent to headphones or another device.

Can sounds keep playing with the screen locked?

Yes. Enabled room sounds and continuous purring can continue when you lock your iPhone or switch apps. The sleep timer fades them out when it ends. Open Sound and tap Stop all to stop playback sooner.

If playback stops unexpectedly, check whether the timer ended or your audio output changed. Reopen CuteCat and enable the sounds again. If it keeps happening, send us your app version and what happened just before the sound stopped.

How do I change my cat or room?

Tap the room name at the top of the screen to open Your companions. Tap a round cat portrait to see its details, then choose it or view its purchase options. Each cat has a matching room and ambient sound. You can also swipe across the room to change between available companions.

How does the sleep timer work?

Tap Sound and choose a duration under Turn sounds off after. The timer fades out all sounds when it ends, including while the screen is locked. Choose Off to cancel the timer, or Stop all to stop the audio and cancel the timer immediately.

Which companions are free?

Kitten, Maine Coon, and British are included. Velvet and Mochi are separate one-time purchases, each with its own room and ambient sound. Neither is a subscription. Optional tips support development and do not unlock companions.

How do I restore Velvet or Mochi?

Open Your companions by tapping the room name, then tap Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the original purchase and connect to the internet. You can also restore from a premium companion's detail screen. If it still appears locked, contact support with the companion name and app version.

What does Preview mean in TestFlight?

TestFlight testers may see Preview Velvet or Preview Mochi. This lets you try that companion for the current session. It does not record a purchase and ends when you close and relaunch CuteCat or choose End TestFlight previews. Purchased access and Restore purchases are separate.

How do Sleep and Wake work?

Tap Sleep to let the room dim and your cat settle down. Tap Wake when you want your cat awake again. The sleep timer controls when sounds stop; putting your cat to sleep does not set a timer automatically.

A tip didn't go through.

Tips are optional. If products do not load, check your internet connection and try again later. A pending purchase may need Apple's or a parent's approval. You can keep using CuteCat while a purchase is pending.

How can I request a refund?

Apple processes in-app purchases. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to check your purchase and request a refund. Eligibility depends on Apple's policies and applicable law.

How can I remove my data?

CuteCat has no account to delete. Cat and room preferences are stored locally. Use Delete App in iOS to remove local app data, and manage backups through your device or Apple settings. To request deletion of support correspondence, email us. More details are in our Privacy Policy.
