Relaxation and entertainment

CuteCat offers a virtual cat, ambient scenes, sounds, and a sleep timer for personal enjoyment. It is not a medical device, treatment, or professional health service. We do not promise a particular sleep or health outcome. Use audio at a comfortable volume and only where it is safe to relax.

Optional tips and purchases

Three companions and their matching rooms are included: Kitten, Maine Coon, and British. Velvet and Mochi are separate optional, one-time, non-consumable purchases. Each unlocks that companion and its matching room. These purchases are not subscriptions and can be restored through Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

The app also offers optional, repeatable tips as consumable in-app purchases. A tip supports development; it does not unlock cats or features and does not create a subscription. Companion purchases are separate from tips. Apple shows the applicable price, currency, and purchase confirmation before payment.

Purchases are subject to availability and Apple's payment terms. For billing questions and refund requests, use Apple's Report a Problem service. Eligibility is determined under Apple's policies and applicable law. Nothing here removes mandatory consumer rights.

Availability and compatibility

CuteCat is being prepared for its first public App Store release. Beta screenshots and previews may differ from the released version. The final App Store listing will state availability, price, and device requirements. A release date is not promised on this website.
