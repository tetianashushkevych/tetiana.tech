Optional tips and purchases

Three companions and their matching rooms are included: Kitten, Maine Coon, and British. Velvet and Mochi are separate optional, one-time, non-consumable purchases. Each unlocks that companion and its matching room. These purchases are not subscriptions and can be restored through Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

The app also offers optional, repeatable tips as consumable in-app purchases. A tip supports development; it does not unlock cats or features and does not create a subscription.
