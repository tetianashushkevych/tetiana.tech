A few little details

Good to know.

Still curious?
We're happy to help.

When can I download CuteCat?

CuteCat is getting ready for its first App Store release. This page will link to the app when it is available. There is no confirmed launch date yet.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The cat, rooms, and included sounds work on your iPhone without a connection. Downloading the app, making optional purchases, and sending a support email require internet access.

Can I use it without making an account?

Yes. CuteCat does not have account registration. You can open the app and spend time with your cat.

Are tips required?

No. The current version includes an optional tip jar through Apple's in-app purchases. Tips do not unlock features and are not subscriptions. Prices and availability are shown by Apple before you confirm a purchase.

Will sounds play when I lock my iPhone?

Keep CuteCat open while listening. Background and lock-screen playback are not supported in the current version.

Which devices will it support?

CuteCat is being prepared for iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Check the App Store listing for final compatibility when it launches.
