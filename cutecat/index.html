A few little details

We're happy to help.

How can I download CuteCat?

CuteCat is preparing for its first public App Store release. It isn’t available to download from the App Store yet. Check download availability below, or ask about early access.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The cat, rooms, and included sounds work on your iPhone without a connection. Downloading the app, making or restoring purchases, and sending a support email require internet access.

Can I use it without making an account?

Yes. CuteCat does not have account registration. You can open the app and spend time with your cat.

Which cats are included?

Kitten, Maine Coon, and British are included with their matching rooms. Velvet and Mochi are available as separate, optional one-time purchases. Each unlocks that companion and its room. There are no subscriptions.

Are tips required?

No. Tips are optional, repeatable purchases that support development. They do not unlock cats or features. Companion purchases are separate. Apple shows the price and currency before you confirm any purchase.

How do I restore a companion?

Tap the room name to open Your companions, then tap Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Restoration applies to purchased companions; tips are consumable purchases.

Will sounds play when I lock my iPhone?

Enabled room sounds and continuous purring can keep playing when you lock your iPhone or switch apps. The sleep timer fades them out when it ends. To stop sooner, open Sound and tap Stop all.

Which devices will it support?

CuteCat is being prepared for iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Check the App Store listing for final compatibility when it launches.
